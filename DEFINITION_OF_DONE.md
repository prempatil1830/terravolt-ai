## 2. Status of Known Engineering Limitations

The following four known limitations are acknowledged and fully disclosed in the documentation:

### 2.1 OSM-Only Grid Data with No Capacity Check
- **Status:** **DISCLOSED & PARTIAL (Design Boundary)**
- **Citation:** `criteria.md § Criterion 4`, `final_report.md § 5.1`, `README.md § 5.1`
- **Impact:** Substation coordinates originate exclusively from OpenStreetMap. While OSM verifies geographic presence and nominal voltage class (≥33 kV), it provides **zero data regarding substation transformer capacity (MVA), busbar fault levels, or injection headroom**. A 33 kV substation may be located 0.5 km away but operating at 100% capacity with zero injection headroom for a 50 MWp plant.
- **Remediation Required:** Formal KPTCL/BESCOM interconnection feasibility filings and Single Line Diagram (SLD) reviews are mandatory prior to financial or land acquisition commitments.

### 2.2 1 x 1 degree grid (about 110 km) GHI Spatial Resolution
- **Status:** **DISCLOSED & ACCEPTED (Data Limitation)**
- **Citation:** `criteria.md § Criterion 1`, `final_report.md § 5.2`, `README.md § 5.2`
- **Impact:** Solar irradiation data from NASA POWER Climatology operates on a **1 x 1 degree grid (about 110 km)** (`ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN`). Temperature operates on a 0.5 x 0.625 degree grid (`T2M`). Candidate sites within ~110 km receive byte-identical GHI values. Consequently, GHI provides minimal discrimination among adjacent candidates in the central solar belt, and micro-climatic terrain shading along the Western Ghats cannot be resolved.
- **Remediation Required:** Site-specific PVGIS-SARAH2 or Solargis time-series data must be acquired for bankable P50/P90 generation modeling.

### 2.3 Unverified 5% Slope Threshold and 4.5 Acres/MWp Footprint
- **Status:** **DISCLOSED & WORKING ASSUMPTION**
- **Citation:** `assumptions.md § 2 & § 7`, `criteria.md § Criterion 2`, `final_report.md § 5.3`
- **Impact:** 
  1. The **5.0% slope threshold** is an industrial engineering rule-of-thumb to minimize civil cut-and-fill grading costs for single-axis tracker racking. It is **not confirmed from peer-reviewed academic literature**; fixed-tilt installations frequently accommodate up to 7–10% slope with civil grading cost adjustments.
  2. The **4.5 acres/MWp factor** (225 acres for 50 MWp, resulting in the 0.54 km radius circular footprint disk) is an unverified industry benchmark based on crystalline-silicon tilt pitch.
- **Remediation Required:** Detailed on-site UAV topographical surveys and geotechnical investigations must confirm racking suitability and grading estimates for candidate parcels.

### 2.4 Unverified Reviewer-Dependent Sites (Needs Review Protocol)
- **Status:** **DISCLOSED & GOVERNED**
- **Citation:** `criteria.md § Review Rules`, `final_report.md § 1`, `final_report.md § 3.1`
- **Impact:** 
  - **Site #4 (Vijayanagara, 15.0°N, 76.1°E):** Primary substation is confirmed `way/251750523` (Itagi, 4.87 km, 220kV/66kV). A closer untagged structure exists at `way/251750524` (Morageri, 2.48 km) without verified voltage tags.
  - **Site #12 (Dharwad, 15.4°N, 75.1°E):** Primary substation is confirmed `way/251093109` (Gopankoppa, 4.76 km, 110kV). A closer untagged structure exists at `way/409304562` (2.29 km) without verified voltage tags.
- **Remediation Required:** Both sites are intentionally flagged as `needs_review` with reasons stored cleanly without emojis. Human reviewers must inspect KPTCL single-line diagrams to determine whether closer structures are usable distribution tap points or non-viable sub-33kV assets before clearing them for commercial development.
